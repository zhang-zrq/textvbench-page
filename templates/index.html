02 Inside the evaluator

Illustrative walkthrough
↳

Correct correspondence does not imply correct text. The evaluator selects existing OCR evidence, including misspellings. It never rewrites that evidence to match the target.

The shared evaluator, at a glance +
Metric definitions & evaluation protocol +

Spatial fidelity

Normalized character edit similarity between a target and its matched OCR text, averaged over active observations. Whitespace and punctuation are removed; case is preserved.

Temporal fidelity

Text consistency between adjacent active observations of the same target. A missing match contributes zero; an inactive carrier/reference is excluded according to the track’s activation rule.

TextVScore

100 × 2ST / (S + T)

Targets are weighted by normalized text length. Ref2V then averages references equally. If S or T is zero, the score is zero.

Separate diagnostics

Extra measures unassigned readable text. Ref2V also reports reading-order preservation. Neither is folded into TextVScore.

Paper protocol: 3 fps, at most 30 sampled frames, deterministic jitter, OCR confidence ≥ 0.80, at most 20 active candidates per frame, and target batches of at most three. T2V uses a 0.95 fast-match threshold. A single valid observation uses its spatial score as temporal fidelity; never-observed targets receive zero.
